# Artur Onysko

jobs, and decision gates. My contribution is turning ambiguous operations into
a small, reviewable implementation with explicit failure handling and proof.

separate permission boundaries.

## Best engagement shape

1. map the workflow and its failure modes;
2. freeze inputs, outputs, authority, and acceptance criteria;
3. deliver one reviewable vertical slice;
4. prove normal and hostile paths;
5. report known limits and the next useful milestone.

Best fit: internal operations automation, AI agent and tool workflows,
integration control planes, and reliability or verification seams.
