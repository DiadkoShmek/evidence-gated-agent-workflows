The deliverable

A narrow reliability layer, not a slide deck.

01

Boundary map

One source, one target, exact ownership, expected schema, authority limits, and measurable acceptance criteria.

02

Fail-closed adapter

A small implementation that rejects missing, stale, malformed, substituted, or contradictory evidence instead of promoting it.

03

Hostile proof

Normal and adversarial tests, decision trace, known limits, and a reproducible handoff your engineers can inspect.

Public, runnable evidence

Inspect the method before buying the sprint.

The repository contains dependency-free references for evidence gates, bounded asynchronous polling, operator handoff, and explicit terminal failure states. One command runs the proof suite.

python3 run_proof.py

What this proves

  • Missing evidence does not become success.
  • Retries and polling have hard bounds.
  • Duplicate effects and ambiguous outcomes are held.
  • Human approval remains explicit.

What it does not prove

Production deployment, client results, security certification, or access to your systems.

Good first use

Bring the handoff your team does not trust.

Examples: an agent result entering an internal tool, a model artifact crossing into a runtime, an async job whose status can lie, or a data receipt that must survive malformed and racing inputs.

If a useful proof cannot be completed inside the agreed boundary, you receive an honest hold report with the exact blocker and reusable artifacts.
